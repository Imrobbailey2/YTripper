This video covers identification of larvae of the subfamily Carpomyini, including the genera Rhagoletis, Carpomya, and Zonosemata. Key characteristics are included with examples.
Dr. Gary Steck is a taxonomic entomologist with the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services and curator of Diptera at the Florida State Collection of Arthropods.
This is part of a series of videos on Tephritidae of economic importance. Lectures were provided by Dr. Norman Barr, Dr. Camiel Doorenweerd, Dr. Luc Leblanc, Dr. Allen Norrbom, and Dr. Gary Steck.
Hosting and filming of the Fruit Fly Taxonomic Training Workshop is possible through the USDA-APHIS Plant Protection Act Section 7721 Funding for 2018, Cooperative Agreement No. AP18PPQS&TT00C146 to the University of Florida. Project coordination was provided by Dr. Amanda Hodges (DPM Director, University of Florida) in association with the University of Florida BRE lab and Platinum Digital Video.
