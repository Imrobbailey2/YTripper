This video is an overview of identification and recognition of adult and larval Tephritidae as well as important pest genera and species.
Allen Norrbom is an internationally recognized expert in the taxonomy of fruit flies and other Diptera families, with 34 years as a research entomologist at the USDA Systematic Entomology Laboratory.
This is part of a series of videos on Tephritidae of economic importance. Resources for identification including LUCID and molecular techniques. Lectures were provided by Dr. Norman Barr, Dr. Camiel Doorenweerd, Dr. Luc Leblanc, Dr. Allen Norrbom, and Dr. Gary Steck.
Hosting and filming of the Fruit Fly Taxonomic Training Workshop is possible through the USDA-APHIS Plant Protection Act Section 7721 Funding for 2018, Cooperative Agreement No. AP18PPQS&TT00C146 to the University of Florida. Project coordination was provided by Dr. Amanda Hodges (DPM Director, University of Florida) in association with the University of Florida BRE lab and Platinum Digital Video.
